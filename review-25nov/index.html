PART 1. TROUSER SHOT LIST (pictures the renderer needs)
======================================================================

SAME SET-UP FOR EVERY PICTURE (so the pictures line up and options can be swapped)
- Cloth: navy, plain (solid), matte, no visible weave or sheen. Same cloth in every picture.
- Garment on a full-length form (legs, or an invisible/ghost form). Trousers only: no jacket, no shoes,
  no belt, nothing tucked in.
- Background: plain white, seamless. Soft, even light, the same light as the jacket pictures.
  No strong shadows, no reflections, no props.
- Camera: front and back straight on at hip height; side at exactly 90 degrees (left side).
  Same distance, zoom and framing for every picture of the same view.
- Framing: whole trousers from waistband to hem, centred, a small even margin all round.
- Size: portrait 2:3, at least 1024 x 1536 (larger is fine if every picture is the same size).
- File: PNG or high-quality JPEG. No text, labels or watermark ON the picture: the name goes in the file name.
